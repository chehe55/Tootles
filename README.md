# Tootles! 🐢

A little turtle, a big adventure! Tootles is a 2D game built with **Godot 4.4 and GDScript**, mixing lane-dodging action, free movement, and animated cutscenes.

## The design

Gameplay switches between moving freely and cruising forward through five lanes. Dodge incoming light balls, steer around roaming crabs, and time your shield to keep your three hearts intact. Simple controls keep things approachable, while faster obstacle waves turn up the challenge!

## Features

- Animated turtles with movement, damage, and shield effects.
- Randomized light-ball patterns and increasingly frequent waves.
- A temporary shield with a cooldown indicator.
- Three-heart health and level restart screens.
- Animated cutscenes, sound effects, and music.

## Controls

- **WASD / Arrow keys:** Move; use up and down to switch lanes.
- **Space:** Activate your shield.

Open the project in **Godot 4.4** and hit **Run**. Tootles! 🐢
